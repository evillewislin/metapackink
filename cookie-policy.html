The short version

This website sets no advertising cookies, no retargeting pixels and no social media trackers. No cookie here follows you around the internet.

We use a single Google Analytics 4 property to understand which pages are useful. Everything else the site needs to work is a strictly necessary item that does not identify you.

The categories, explained

We group what we use into two categories, because the distinction matters for what you can switch off.

  • Strictly necessary. These make the site work: remembering your chosen language and remembering that you have already dismissed any notice. They do not identify you, and the site cannot function properly without them, so they do not require consent.
  • Analytics. Google Analytics 4 tells us which pages are read, which are abandoned and roughly where visitors come from — in aggregate. It does not give us your name, and we do not enable Google Signals or advertising features. You can block this without affecting the site.

How to control or opt out

You are in control of all of it. Any of these will work:

  • Browser settings. Every major browser lets you block or delete cookies and clear site data. Blocking strictly necessary items may make parts of the site behave oddly, but blocking analytics has no effect on how it works.
  • Private or incognito mode. Cookies set during a private session are discarded when you close the window.
  • Google Analytics opt-out. Google publishes a browser add-on that stops Google Analytics collecting data on any site you visit.
  • Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. We honour these signals as an opt-out of analytics wherever the browser sends them.

Third-party services this site interacts with

Two third parties are relevant:

  • Google Analytics 4 — loads on page view to count and describe visits in aggregate. It receives your IP address and technical data about your device and browser. Google acts as our processor for this.
  • Cloudflare — hosts and serves this website, and processes request data as part of delivering it, including basic security and performance logging.

Changes to this policy

If we add a cookie or change what an existing one does, we will update the table above and revise the date at the top of this page. We will not introduce advertising or cross-site tracking cookies without telling you on this page first.
