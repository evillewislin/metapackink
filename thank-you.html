We reply to every enquiry within one business day. If your brief is complete enough for us to quote against, that reply will normally include structure and material recommendations with an indicative price.

What happens next

  1. We review your specification. If something is unclear or a detail would change the recommendation, we will ask rather than guess.
  2. You receive a quotation. Structure and material recommendations with indicative pricing, normally within one business day.
  3. We produce a physical sample. Once structure and finishing are agreed, for your written approval.
  4. Approved samples go into production with in-line quality checks and a pre-shipment final inspection.
