ONE PROJECT / ONE WORKFLOW

Packaging should be engineered, not simply printed.

A successful packaging project involves more than artwork. The structure, board, paper, finishing, internal protection, assembly and logistics all affect the final result.

Metapackink can coordinate these requirements around your product specifications and target presentation.

OUR PROCESS

How a custom packaging project works.

01

Project Brief

Send your product dimensions, quantity, target market, packaging references and project requirements.

02

Structural Development

We determine the appropriate box construction, dimensions and material requirements for your project.

03

Materials & Finishing

Select board, paper, fabrics, coatings, foil, embossing, debossing, spot UV and other finishes.

04

Artwork & Prototype

Confirm dielines, artwork, construction and physical sample requirements before production.

05

Mass Production

Approved specifications are transferred into production with quality checks throughout the process.

06

Inspection & Delivery

Finished packaging is checked before packing and shipment to the requested destination.
