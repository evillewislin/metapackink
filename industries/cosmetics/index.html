COSMETICS & BEAUTY

Custom cosmetic packaging for beauty brands

Packaging for skincare, makeup, personal-care and beauty gift sets — developed around the product it holds, the shelf it sits on and the journey it has to survive.

Cosmetic Packaging Applications

Custom packaging can be developed for skincare products, facial care products, makeup products, beauty products, personal-care products, cosmetic gift sets and premium beauty collections.

Custom Cosmetic Box Structures

Depending on product and presentation requirements, options may include rigid boxes, magnetic boxes, two-piece boxes, drawer boxes, premium presentation boxes and customized box structures.

Materials & Finishing

Potential options include greyboard, coated paper, specialty paper, textured paper, foil stamping, embossing, debossing, spot UV and lamination.

Packaging for Premium Beauty Brands

Packaging can support brand identity, shelf presentation, product protection, premium appearance, opening experience and gift presentation.

From Product Dimensions to Production

A custom cosmetic packaging project can follow Requirements → Structure → Material → Sample → Approval → Production → QC.
