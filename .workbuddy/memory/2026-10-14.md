## Conventions reinforced

- Slot-insert geometry that the jewellery case study documents: a slit holds a ring by **wedging at its ends**, not by face compression. Wedge depth `d` solves `2·sqrt(2R·d − d²) = L`, so the slit must be **shorter than the smallest item's outer diameter** or the item falls through to the floor of the slot.
