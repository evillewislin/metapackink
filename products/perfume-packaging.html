PERFUME PACKAGING

Perfume Packaging

Custom fragrance boxes built around bottle dimensions. Rigid and magnetic structures with inserts designed to hold the bottle securely.

Request a quote Request a sample

Perfume packaging is a dimensional problem before it is a design problem. The bottle has a specific footprint and height, the cap is often wider than the body, and the whole thing has to arrive without moving inside the box. Get the cavity wrong and no amount of finishing rescues it.

So we work from the bottle. Send the bottle dimensions — or the bottle itself — and the box and insert are developed around it, with the clearance set so the bottle is held firmly but can be taken out without force.

What we design around

Bottle footprint and height

Including any cap or atomiser that is wider than the bottle body, which is the most common fit error we correct.

Bottle weight

Glass is heavy per unit volume; the board thickness and cavity depth follow the weight, not just the dimensions.

Cavity clearance

Set so the bottle cannot rattle in transit but still lifts out cleanly. Too tight is worse than too loose — it risks chipping the glass.

Gift set configuration

Single bottle, bottle plus a smaller travel size, or a full set with a printed insert card.

Construction options

  • Rigid lid-and-base — the classic fragrance box, with a die-cut fitment holding the bottle.
  • Magnetic closure — hinged lid with concealed magnets, common for premium and gift-set fragrance.
  • Drawer — sliding tray, used for sets and for display-led packshots.
  • Fitment — die-cut paperboard collar, foam or EVA cavity, or a moulded insert for irregular bottle shapes.
  • Neck support — where a tall bottle needs the neck or the cap restrained as well as the base.

Finishing

Foil stamping

The dominant finish on fragrance packaging — brand marks, borders and fine detail in metallic or specialty foil.

Emboss and deboss

Tactile brand marks, often combined with foil on the same element.

Soft-touch lamination

A velvety matte surface that is standard across premium fragrance.

Spot UV

Gloss detail layered over a matte, soft-touch surface.

Specialty and textured papers

Heavy textured stock for a tactile surface under the hand.

Ribbon

Colour-matched satin ribbon as a pull or as a decorative band.

Questions about this packaging

What bottle dimensions do you need?

Overall height including the cap, the bottle body width and depth, the widest point if the cap or shoulders are wider than the body, and the filled weight. If the cap is wider than the bottle body, tell us — that is the most common cause of a box that does not close properly.

Can you work from a bottle sample?

Yes, and it is the most reliable route. Send the physical bottle or a filled sample and the cavity is developed and tested against it, so the fit is confirmed before production rather than discovered afterwards.

How is the bottle held in place?

Normally a die-cut paperboard collar that grips the bottle at the base, sometimes with a neck or cap support for tall bottles. Foam and EVA cavities are used where the bottle shape is irregular or the protection requirement is higher.

Will the bottle rattle in transit?

Not if the cavity is specified correctly. Clearance is set tight enough to hold the bottle firmly without making it difficult to remove — the tension is checked on the physical sample with the actual bottle fitted.

Do you make multi-bottle gift sets?

Yes. Compartmented fitments for a full-size bottle plus a travel size, or a complete set with a printed insert card, are common configurations.
