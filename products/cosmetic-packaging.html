COSMETIC PACKAGING

Cosmetic Packaging

Branded packaging for skincare and beauty products. Structures developed around jars, bottles, tubes and multi-piece routine sets.

Request a quote Request a sample

Cosmetic packaging spans a wider range of container shapes than almost any other category — a heavy glass cream jar, a slim serum dropper, a wide compact, a tube, a pump bottle — and each one has different fit and protection requirements inside the same box.

So the insert does most of the work. We design the cavity around the specific container, and where a set mixes shapes we build a compartmented fitment so every piece is held individually rather than loose in the same space.

What we design around

Mixed container shapes

A routine set with a jar, a dropper bottle and a tube needs three different cavities in one box.

Heavy glass

Cream jars and glass bottles carry real weight; board thickness and cavity depth follow it.

Retail shelf requirements

Boxes that have to stack squarely, face out cleanly and survive handling on a retail floor.

E-commerce transit

Packaging that ships direct to the customer has to protect through a courier network, not just look good on a shelf.

Construction options

  • Rigid lid-and-base — the standard presentation box for a single hero product.
  • Magnetic closure — common for gift sets, discovery sets and premium skincare.
  • Drawer — sliding tray for sets and for products presented as a reveal.
  • Folding carton — for lighter cosmetic items where a rigid structure is unnecessary.
  • Compartmented fitment — individual cavities so multi-piece sets do not move against each other.
  • Leaflet and mirror — printed insert cards, leaflets and mirror inserts as part of the assembly.

Finishing

Foil stamping

Brand marks and fine decorative detail in metallic or specialty foil.

Spot UV

Gloss pattern over matte, widely used across beauty packaging.

Soft-touch lamination

A velvety matte surface that photographs well and feels premium.

Emboss and deboss

Tactile brand marks, often combined with foil.

Printed wrap

Full-colour artwork, including photographic and gradient reproduction.

Specialty papers

Textured and coated stocks for a distinctive surface under the hand.

Questions about this packaging

Can you make packaging for a mixed-format skincare set?

Yes, and it is one of the most common requests. Each container gets its own cavity in a compartmented fitment, so a heavy glass jar and a thin dropper bottle are both held securely without moving against each other in transit.

What do you need to design the insert?

The dimensions of each container, and ideally a physical sample of each. For cream jars the filled weight matters as much as the dimensions, because it determines how deep the cavity has to be to hold the jar without straining the base.

Is rigid packaging necessary for cosmetics?

Not always. Lighter items and refill pouches often work fine in a folding carton at a much lower unit cost. Rigid and magnetic structures earn their cost when presentation, protection or product weight justify them — we will tell you which side of that line your product falls on.

Can the box ship direct to consumers?

Yes, though it needs designing for it. E-commerce transit is harsher than retail pallet transit, so the cavity needs to hold through a courier network. Tell us it is shipping direct and we will specify the fitment accordingly.

Do you supply the insert card and leaflet too?

Yes — printed insert cards, leaflets and mirror inserts are produced as part of the assembly, so the box arrives ready to pack rather than needing components from a second supplier.
