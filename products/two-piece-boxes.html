TWO-PIECE RIGID BOXES

Two-Piece Rigid Boxes

Classic lid-and-base construction with custom finishing. A separate lid and base held by friction — the simplest premium structure there is.

Request a quote Request a sample

A two-piece rigid box is exactly what it sounds like: a base and a separate lid that slides on and holds by friction. There is no hinge, no magnet and no mechanism, which makes it the most direct and often the most economical way to present a product in a rigid box.

It is also the structure with the clearest "ker-chunk" of assembly: the lid seats fully home, which gives the customer a definite sense that the box is closed.

When a two-piece box is the right choice

You want premium without a mechanism

Rigid presentation and the feel of a proper box, without the cost of a hinge, magnet set or lid wrap.

The budget is a real constraint

The simplest rigid construction, so the per-unit cost is lower than a magnetic or book-style box at the same size.

The box needs to be produced at scale

Fewer components and simpler assembly make it the easiest rigid structure to run in high volume.

Classic retail presentation

Beauty, confectionery, apparel and premium retail where the lid-lifts-off gesture is the expected one.

Construction options

  • Lid depth — full-depth lid covering the whole base, or a shallow lid that shows a band of the base as a design detail.
  • Lid fit — lid-to-base clearance is set so the lid holds by friction but still releases without force. Too tight and it sticks; too loose and it falls off.
  • Board — greyboard 1.5 mm to 2.5 mm, matched to box size.
  • Base interior — die-cut fitment, foam or EVA cavity, or a simple printed base liner.

Finishing

Full wrap

Lid and base wrapped in the same stock for a continuous look.

Contrasting wraps

A different stock, colour or finish on the lid and the base to create a deliberate two-tone effect.

Foil stamping

Foil on the lid face, lid edge or base as a brand mark.

Emboss and deboss

Tactile branding, often on the lid face where the thumb naturally lands.

Spot UV

Selective gloss against matte for surface contrast.

Printed wrap

Full-colour print with your artwork across one or both pieces.

Questions about this packaging

Will the lid stay on in transit?

Yes, provided the lid fit is specified correctly. The lid-to-base clearance is set so the lid holds by friction — we test it on the physical sample, because a lid that holds well on a bench can behave differently once the base carries the product weight.

Can the lid and base be different colours?

Yes, and it is a common design choice. A contrasting lid and base creates a deliberate two-tone effect and is often cheaper than adding a printed design.

Two-piece or magnetic — which should I choose?

Two-piece is simpler, cheaper and easier to scale; magnetic opens more deliberately and stays shut more positively. If the unboxing moment matters and the budget allows, magnetic; if you want premium presentation at the best unit cost, two-piece.

Can I add an insert?

Yes. A die-cut paperboard fitment, foam or EVA cavity, or a printed base liner are all normal additions, and the fitment is designed alongside the box so the product sits correctly.
