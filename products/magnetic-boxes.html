MAGNETIC CLOSURE BOXES

Magnetic Closure Boxes

Premium magnetic boxes for luxury product presentation. A concealed magnet holds the lid shut, so the box opens deliberately rather than falling open.

Request a quote Request a sample

A magnetic closure box is a rigid box with magnets set into the lid and the base so the lid snaps down and holds. The magnets are concealed inside the board, so nothing is visible from the outside — the closure simply feels intentional, and the box stays shut in a bag or on a shelf.

It is the structure most often chosen for unboxing-led products and gift sets, because the moment of opening is a designed moment rather than an accident of gravity.

When a magnetic box is the right choice

The unboxing is the point

Brands where opening the package is a designed experience the customer films, photographs or repeats.

The box travels and returns

The magnetic lid keeps the box shut in a bag, and the rigid structure survives being kept and reused.

Gift and presentation sets

Multi-piece gift sets, beauty sets and subscription boxes where a hinged lid reads as considered.

It needs to stay shut

Products handled or displayed by the customer, where a loose lid would open at the wrong moment.

Construction options

  • Magnet specification — magnet size and grade are chosen against the lid size and how firmly the box should hold. Undersized magnets let the lid drift; oversized ones make it fight the customer.
  • Magnet placement — concealed between the board and the wrap, set in recesses so the surface stays flat with no bulge.
  • Lid style — full flap, half flap, or a wrap-around cover wrapping onto the base.
  • Opening — ribbon pull, die-cut thumb tab, or a lift-off lid with no tab.
  • Interior — die-cut fitment, foam or EVA cavity, compartmented tray for multi-product sets.

Finishing

Foil stamping

Brand mark or logo in metallic or specialty foil on the lid face or the spine.

Emboss and deboss

Tactile branding that reads in the hand as well as the eye.

Spot UV

Gloss pattern against matte for a layered, premium surface.

Soft-touch lamination

A velvety surface finish that is common on premium beauty and fragrance packaging.

Printed wrap

Full-colour printed paper or specialty textured stock with your artwork.

Ribbon

Satin or grosgrain ribbon pull, colour-matched to the brand palette.

Questions about this packaging

How strong should the magnets be?

Strong enough to hold the lid closed in handling, but not so strong that the customer has to pry it open. Magnet size and grade are specified against the lid dimensions and the holding force the box needs, which is why lid size matters more than the product weight here.

Can the magnets be hidden?

Yes, and they normally are. The magnets sit in recesses between the board and the wrap so the outside of the box stays perfectly flat with no bulge or visible closure.

What is the difference between a magnetic box and a two-piece box?

A two-piece box is a separate lid and base held by friction and gravity; a magnetic box has a hinged lid held by magnets. Magnetic boxes cost more, open more deliberately, and stay shut in transit. See the comparison for the full trade-off.

Can I add a ribbon pull?

Yes. A colour-matched satin or grosgrain ribbon pull is one of the most common additions, either as a lift tab or as a decorative band under the lid.

Will the lid stay aligned after repeated opening?

The rigid shell and the board hinge are what keep alignment. We build the hinge and lid clearance so the box still closes cleanly after repeated use, which matters for packaging that is kept rather than discarded.
