CUSTOM DRAWER BOXES

Custom Drawer Boxes

Elegant pull-out drawer packaging for premium retail and gifting. An inner tray slides out of a rigid outer sleeve.

Request a quote Request a sample

A drawer box is a rigid outer sleeve with a sliding inner tray. Pull the ribbon or the finger hole and the tray comes out, presenting the product as a revealed object rather than something lifted out of a lid.

The structure suits products that are displayed as much as they are opened, and it gives designers a clean, unbroken outer surface to print on — the sleeve can carry the artwork with nothing interrupting it.

When a drawer box is the right choice

The product should be revealed

Jewellery, accessories, small premium goods, where sliding the drawer open presents the product cleanly.

The outer face is the design

A seamless sleeve gives an uninterrupted printed or wrapped surface, with no lid seam or hinge to design around.

Retail shelf presence

A drawer box stacks squarely and presents a clean face on shelf, which a hinged lid does not always do.

Repeated handling

Drawer boxes are opened and closed often; the sliding action stays reliable where a friction lid loosens with use.

Construction options

  • Sleeve — rigid greyboard sleeve wrapped in paper or fabric, carrying the outer design.
  • Tray — sliding inner tray, wrapped to match or deliberately contrasted.
  • Pull — ribbon pull, die-cut finger hole, or a tab formed from the tray itself.
  • Stop — an internal stop so the tray cannot be pulled fully out unless the design intends it.
  • Interior — die-cut fitment, foam or EVA cavity inside the tray.

Finishing

Foil stamping

Brand mark on the sleeve face, where it reads on shelf.

Emboss and deboss

Tactile detail on the sleeve or the tray face.

Spot UV

Gloss pattern across a matte sleeve for surface contrast.

Printed sleeve

Full-colour artwork wrapping the whole outer face uninterrupted.

Ribbon pull

Satin or grosgrain ribbon, colour-matched, as the drawer pull.

Contrast linings

A different stock inside the tray for a reveal effect when it opens.

Questions about this packaging

Can the drawer be pulled out completely?

That is a design decision. Most drawer boxes have an internal stop so the tray cannot be pulled fully out and dropped. If you want the tray to come free — for example as a separate display element — we build it without the stop.

How does the drawer slide?

The tray runs on the inside face of the sleeve, with the clearance specified so it moves smoothly without binding. Board thickness, wrap thickness and humidity all affect the fit, which is why the sliding action is checked on the physical sample rather than assumed.

What is the best pull option?

A ribbon pull reads most premium and allows the sleeve to stay completely clean when closed. A die-cut finger hole is more robust and needs no extra component, but it interrupts the outer face.

Can the sleeve be printed all over?

Yes. The wrap can carry full-colour artwork uninterrupted across the whole sleeve, which is the main design advantage of a drawer box over a hinged lid.
