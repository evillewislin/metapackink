MANUFACTURING PROCESS

From your brief to a shipped box

Ten stages, in the order they actually happen. Knowing where a project is — and what has to be signed off before the next stage starts — is what keeps a packaging order on schedule.

01

Project Requirements

Product dimensions, weight, quantity, application, artwork, reference images, materials and finishing requirements are reviewed.

02

Structural Development

The packaging structure is developed around product dimensions, opening method, presentation, material thickness, stability, assembly and shipping considerations.

03

Material Selection

Materials are selected according to the required structure, appearance and application, including greyboard, specialty papers, coated papers, textured papers and other packaging materials.

04

Quotation

The project is reviewed according to structure, dimensions, materials, quantity, printing, finishing, accessories and shipping requirements.

05

Prototype & Sampling

Samples can be used to confirm dimensions, structure, materials, artwork, printing, finishing, product fit and overall presentation.

06

Artwork & Production Specification

Artwork, materials, dimensions, printing, finishing and assembly specifications are finalized before mass production.

07

Mass Production

Production can involve board preparation, printing, mounting, die cutting, folding, box forming, magnetic assembly, finishing and interior assembly.

08

Printing & Finishing

Finishing options may include foil stamping, embossing, debossing, spot UV, lamination and specialty papers.

09

Quality Control

Production checks are used to verify important packaging specifications before shipment.

10

Packing & Shipment

Finished packaging is packed according to agreed requirements and prepared for shipment.
