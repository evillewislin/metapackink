About METAPACKINK

METAPACKINK is a China-based custom packaging manufacturer serving international brands and businesses that require packaging developed around their products, brand identity and production requirements.

What Does METAPACKINK Manufacture?

METAPACKINK focuses on premium paper-based packaging and customized presentation boxes. Our main packaging categories include custom rigid boxes, magnetic closure boxes, two-piece rigid boxes, custom drawer boxes, perfume packaging, cosmetic packaging, premium gift packaging and branded product presentation boxes.

Packaging Developed Around Your Product

Every product has different dimensions, presentation requirements and packaging objectives. Packaging can be developed around product dimensions, product weight, brand identity, retail presentation, opening experience, protection requirements, shipping considerations, materials, printing and finishing.

From Structure to Production

Custom packaging projects can involve multiple stages. METAPACKINK can support requirement review, structural development, material selection, quotation, prototype or sample development, artwork review, sample approval, production, printing and finishing, quality control, packing and shipment.

Packaging for International Buyers

METAPACKINK works with businesses that need customized packaging manufactured in China, including consumer brands, cosmetic brands, perfume brands, beauty companies, retail businesses, product manufacturers, gift companies, importers, distributors and packaging procurement teams.

Our Manufacturing Approach

METAPACKINK combines structural development, material selection, printing, finishing and production control within a customized packaging workflow. The process begins with understanding the product and packaging requirements before determining an appropriate structure, material and finishing specification.
