Perfume Packaging Applications

Custom perfume packaging can be developed for eau de parfum, eau de toilette, fragrance collections, premium perfume products, limited-edition fragrances and perfume gift sets.

Custom Perfume Box Structures

Depending on bottle dimensions and presentation requirements, perfume packaging may use rigid perfume boxes, magnetic perfume boxes, two-piece rigid boxes, drawer boxes and custom presentation boxes.

Bottle Fit & Internal Configuration

Packaging requirements may include bottle dimensions, bottle weight, internal clearance, product positioning, opening experience, protection during handling and presentation requirements.

Premium Printing & Finishing

Options may include foil stamping, embossing, debossing, spot UV, lamination, specialty papers and textured materials.
